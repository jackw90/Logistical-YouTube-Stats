How Often Does This Update?

YouTube API: GitHub Actions requests fresh statistics every hour at 7 minutes past the hour, UTC. This means 7 minutes past the hour in winter and 7 minutes past the following hour in British Summer Time.

Website: Checks the published data every 60 seconds. Refreshing the page does not force a new YouTube API request.

Data freshness: Subscriber counts may be rounded by YouTube, and published statistics can lag behind YouTube Studio. GitHub's scheduled jobs can also run later than expected.
